A few useful answers.

Do the people I invite need an AI agent?

No. Viewers and editors open the app in their browser and sign in. Developers use their own connected agent to change the software.

What does Admin access allow?

Admin access gives a trusted developer full control of a tool, including its source, secrets, sharing, and deletion. Give editors access when they only need to work with the app’s data.

How is my data protected?

Boat House provides HTTPS, app access controls, separate app databases, and nightly backups on the hosted service. Read our security page for the current controls and limitations before deciding what data to store.

Can I move an existing app over?

Yes. Give your agent the app’s source and ask it to deploy to Boat House. Your agent may need to adapt the app and migrate its data. The documentation explains the supported deployment format.
