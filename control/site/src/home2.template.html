Questions people ask.

I do not know what JSON is. Is this for me?

Yes. You are exactly who this was built for: someone with real work to do, an agent that can write the software, and no wish to learn what a port number is. You say what you want in ordinary sentences and your agent handles the rest of the vocabulary. If you can write a clear email to a colleague, you can run software on Boat House.

What if my agent breaks the tool?

It will, eventually, and that is fine. Every change to the software can be undone with one sentence, and the previous version comes back the way it was. Your data is copied every night on top of that, and one more sentence puts it back from last night's copy. This is why undo and nightly backups are included instead of sold separately.

Who do I call when something breaks?

Email support@example.com. Every email is answered within one business day, by the person who built this. Before that, your agent can read the tool's logs and put back any earlier version itself, which fixes most things in a minute.

How do my people sign in? Does it work on a phone?

You give your agent each person's email once. They get an email with a link that works for seven days; they open it, choose a password, and they are in. Sign-in and the tools themselves work in any phone browser, and a sign-in lasts a year, so nobody types a password twice a day. Editors can change what is inside a tool, such as logging their hours; viewers can only look; admins can change the software itself, from their own agent. People you share with may be under 18, but not under 13.

I already have a tool running on my laptop. Is it really one sentence?

Almost. Your agent has to swap whatever the tool saves to a local file for the database we give it, put uploads in the data folder, and import your existing rows once. That is one agent session, usually well under an hour, not one sentence. After that, every change is one sentence.

Who owns the software?

You do. The code, the data, the files and any domain you buy are yours. Claude, Codex and Lovable write software; they do not own it, and neither does Boat House. bh export gives you one file with everything, and a note on how to run it elsewhere, any time you like.

Can my co-founder use a different AI than me?

Yes. One of you on Claude Code, the other on Codex or Cursor, both working on the same tool, each from their own agent, with one shared version history. Boat House talks to any agent that speaks MCP, so the choice of AI is yours, and changeable.

Is there a referral program?

Yes, and it is the only discount we have. Every person on Boat House has a code. Sign up with someone's code and every tool is half price for your first 60 days. Give your code to others and you get 10% of what they pay for usage, in cash, every month, for as long as they pay. Your code is on your workspace page and in bh referral.

What happens if I run out of money on the balance?

Your tools pause and stop being reachable. Nothing is deleted and nothing is lost. Put credit back on the balance and everything comes back, data and all. And nothing is spent in the first place without showing you a price and waiting for your yes.

You are one person. What happens to my tools if you stop?

Your data is yours today, not only if we stop. bh export downloads any tool as one file: its code, its database, its files, and a note on how to run it anywhere with a Dockerfile. If Boat House ever winds down, you get notice and that same export of every tool before anything is turned off.
